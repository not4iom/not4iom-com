The Mooir Vannin Issues List

A reminder of the key issues · print it out, take it with you

The twelve key issues with Ørsted's proposed Mooir Vannin offshore wind farm, in brief. Keep it handy, share it, and take it with you when you speak to others.

The printable one-page list. PDF · designed to print

Download & print the list (PDF) →

  1. Revenue for the Isle of Man is unguaranteed and unverified.

  2. Manx taxpayers could face a potential decommissioning bill of up to £740 million.

  3. Cheaper electricity is not guaranteed by Ørsted or Manx Utilities.

  4. The Island Link and grid upgrades are uncosted.

  5. Ørsted now commits to only 5–10 Isle of Man jobs per phase, not 30.

  6. Manx Wildlife Trust and Manx Bat Group have raised the alarm on significant bird and bat deaths.

  7. The Isle of Man's tourism industry is put at risk.

  8. Our UNESCO Biosphere status is potentially at risk.

  9. Serious objections from the Steam Packet Company and Stena Line remain unresolved.

  10. Serious objections from the Ministry of Defence remain unresolved.

  11. Ørsted is seeking approval for a back-up plan, "Scheme 2", that has never been subject to a planning application.

  12. Mooir Vannin could be one of up to six more giant offshore wind farms around the Island.
